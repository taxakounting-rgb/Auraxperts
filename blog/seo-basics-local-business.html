SEO Basics: How Local Businesses Can Rank on Google

AuraXperts Team  ·  4 min read

Search engine optimisation can feel complicated, but local businesses can make real progress with a handful of consistent habits. Here's where to start.

Claim your Google Business Profile

This is the single most important step for local search. Add accurate hours, categories, photos, services and your website link. Keep it updated and post regularly.

Collect and reply to reviews

Ask happy customers to leave a review and reply to every one — positive or negative. Reviews influence both rankings and buying decisions.

Keep your business details consistent

Your name, address and phone number should be identical on your website, Google profile, social media and directories.

Create a page for each service

A single "Services" page rarely ranks well. Give each main service its own page that explains what you offer, who it's for and how to contact you.

Target the words your customers use

Think like a customer: "web design company in Lahore" or "best dentist near me". Use these phrases naturally in titles, headings and text.

Make your site fast and mobile-friendly

Google uses mobile performance as a ranking signal. A slow site loses both rankings and customers.

Publish helpful content

Answer the questions customers ask you every day. Each helpful article is another way for people to find you.

Be patient and measure

SEO compounds over months, not days. Track progress in Google Search Console and keep improving.

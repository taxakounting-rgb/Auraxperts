The 10-Point Website Checklist Every Small Business Needs

AuraXperts Team  ·  4 min read

Your website is often the first impression a customer has of your business. Before spending money on ads or social media, make sure the site can actually turn visitors into enquiries. Use this checklist to find quick wins.

1. It loads in under three seconds

Most visitors leave if a page takes too long. Compress images, use modern formats like WebP, and choose good hosting. Test your site with Google PageSpeed Insights.

2. It works perfectly on mobile

The majority of traffic is mobile. Check that text is readable without zooming, buttons are easy to tap and nothing scrolls sideways.

3. Visitors know what you do within five seconds

Your headline should say who you help and how. Avoid vague slogans — be specific.

4. There is a clear call to action

Every page should guide visitors to one next step: call, WhatsApp, book a consultation or request a quote.

5. Contact details are easy to find

Put your phone number, email and WhatsApp in the header or footer, plus a simple contact form.

6. It shows proof you can be trusted

Real client reviews, project examples, certifications and logos of businesses you've worked with all build confidence.

7. It uses HTTPS

The padlock in the browser matters for trust and for Google rankings. Most modern hosts provide free SSL certificates.

8. Basic SEO is in place

Every page needs a unique title and meta description, one clear H1 heading and descriptive image alt text.

9. Analytics is installed

Set up Google Analytics and Google Search Console so you can see where visitors come from and what they do.

10. Content is up to date

Old prices, outdated services or a copyright year from years ago make a business look inactive. Review your content every quarter.
